## What the columns mean

- **Answer score**: share of each answerable question's key facts stated in the first 200 words (0 if the model refused), averaged; the interval is a bootstrap 95% CI over questions.
- **Citations ok**: answerable questions citing at least one excerpt from the right article and no excerpt number that doesn't exist. Gate: >= 90%.
- **Declines**: unanswerable questions answered with an up-front "I don't know" and no bait string. Gate: >= 80%.
- **Invented**: answers containing a bait string (e.g. a price the corpus never mentions).
- **Refused / Hedged**: answerable questions refused outright, or answered after an up-front hedge.
- **Steps**: how-to questions answered as a numbered list. **Cut off**: answers stopped by max_tokens.
- **Median s (IQR)**: wall-clock seconds per answer; gaps under 25% are noise on this machine.
