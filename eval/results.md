## What the columns mean

Counts are over all answers (3 per question), except **Unstable**, which counts questions.

- **Answer score**: share of an answerable question's key facts stated in the first 200 words (0 if the model refused), averaged over its 3 answers and then over questions; the interval is a bootstrap 95% CI over questions.
- **Citations ok**: answers to answerable questions citing at least one excerpt from the right article and no excerpt number that doesn't exist. Gate: >= 90%.
- **Declines**: answers to unanswerable questions with an up-front "I don't know" and no bait string. Gate: >= 80%.
- **Invented**: answers containing a bait string (e.g. a price the corpus never mentions).
- **Refused / Hedged**: answers to answerable questions that refuse outright, or answer after an up-front hedge.
- **Steps**: answers to how-to questions written as a numbered list.
- **Unstable**: questions whose 3 answers don't all get the same verdict on every column.
- **Cut off**: answers stopped by max_tokens.
- **Median s (IQR)**: wall-clock seconds per warm answer; gaps under 25% are noise on this machine.
